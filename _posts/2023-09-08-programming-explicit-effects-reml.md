My contribution *Programming with Explicit Effects in ReML* was accepted for
presentation at the ML Family Workshop 2023 in Seattle on September 8.

The talk introduces ReML, a functional language that lets programmers be
explicit about memory regions and the effects of their code. These annotations
give the compiler promises to check, making memory behaviour less vulnerable
to changes elsewhere in a program or in its optimiser. ReML builds on MLKit's
region inference, so programmers can combine this control with the convenience
of inferred types and effects.

[Conference programme and abstract](https://icfp23.sigplan.org/details/mlworkshop-2023/5/Programming-with-Explicit-Effects-in-ReML).
